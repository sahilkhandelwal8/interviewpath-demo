# Case-study product brief

Zamp is the company whose hiring assessment this workspace supports. The product described below is the product from the case study; it is not Zamp's own product.

## Product positioning

The case-study product is a recruiting platform for growing software companies. It combines applicant tracking, interview coordination, and hiring analytics in one place. Teams may also connect an existing ATS and use the product's coordination and analytics capabilities.

## Target customer and buyer

- **Customer:** Growing software companies with internal recruiting teams and multi-stage interview processes.
- **Primary buyer:** Head of Talent Acquisition or Recruiting Operations leader.

## Core capabilities

### Applicant tracking

Create job openings, maintain candidate applications, track hiring stages, collect interviewer feedback, and record human decisions.

### Interview coordination

Schedule interviews using interviewer availability and manage rescheduling.

### Feedback follow-up

Send reminders for outstanding interviewer feedback.

### Funnel visibility

Show each candidate's current stage, time spent there, and outstanding next steps.

### Aggregated analytics

Report on stage conversion rates, time in stage, and scheduling or feedback delays.

### Retrospective feedback analysis

Examine recorded interviewer feedback and selection or rejection reasons to surface recurring themes, with references to the underlying feedback.

## Product usage models

1. **Full platform:** Manage candidate applications, coordination, feedback, and analytics in Zamp's ATS.
2. **Connected offering:** Retain an existing ATS and connect it for coordination and analytics. Available functionality depends on the connection's supported data and permissions.

## Expected value

- Less manual interview coordination
- Clearer visibility into the hiring funnel
- Better information for teams reflecting on their hiring process and recorded outcomes

## Product boundaries

The product does not source candidates, manage long-term candidate relationships, rank candidates, independently assess suitability, or make hiring decisions.

Feedback analysis summarizes recorded reasons and patterns. It does not determine whether a decision was correct or establish reasons absent from the records.

## Outreach objective

Start a conversation about a prospect's hiring workflow, interview coordination, funnel visibility, or process-review needs, grounded in relevant public evidence.

## Internal outreach drafting rules

These rules are internal product context and must not be reproduced in prospect-facing messages.

- Select the capability most relevant to the prospect's verified circumstances; do not list every feature.
- Distinguish public facts from possible implications. Ask about potential needs rather than asserting unverified internal problems.
- Describe benefits as intended value, not guaranteed results.
- Do not invent customer references, testimonials, quantified results, pricing, or implementation timelines.
- Discuss ATS and calendar connectivity only in general terms. Do not name supported providers, promise compatibility, or claim seamless integration without separately verified product information.
- Do not assume the prospect wants to replace its ATS. Choose the full-platform or connected-offering angle only when supported by context; otherwise focus on the relevant workflow.
- Describe feedback analysis as reflection on recorded feedback and outcomes, not automated candidate evaluation.
- End with a relevant, low-pressure invitation to discuss the prospect's process.
